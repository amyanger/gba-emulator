# HQ music

Most GBA games, Pokemon included, play their music through Nintendo's MP2K
sound driver. The game mixes every instrument into one 8-bit stream at about
13 kHz, and that low-quality mix is where most of the grainy, metallic sound
comes from. HQ music replays the same notes, instruments, volumes, panning and
reverb, but mixes them in high precision at 32 kHz with smooth interpolation.

Turn it on with **H** while playing, or in **Settings > Audio**. The setting is
remembered. Sound effects made by the GBA's tone channels (menu beeps, parts of
some cries) play exactly as before.

HQ music works with games that use the standard MP2K driver. For other games
the switch shows "Not available for this game" and audio stays as it is.

It only changes what you hear. The game itself runs exactly the same, so save
states, saves and speed are unaffected.
